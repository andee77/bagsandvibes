# CBGV group planning fee: refund policy

Status: **approved by LaDon Headen (CBGV), recorded 2026-10-08.** Source text for the Terms page linked from the site footer (to be built later). Wording may be polished for the Terms page; the rules below may not change without CBGV's approval.

## Who this fee belongs to
The group planning fee is charged by **Checked Bags & Good Vibes (CBGV)** for organising and coordinating the group. It is **not** part of the cruise fare and is **not** a travel agent or cruise line charge. Cruise and other supplier payments are made directly to the cruise line or supplier, under that supplier's own terms.

The same policy applies to **every type of trip** (cruise, resort, villa, destination, wedding, party, corporate).

## Refunds when you cancel
| When you cancel | Refund of the planning fee |
|---|---|
| Within **7 days of paying** the planning fee, **if the trip starts 120 days or more after you paid** | **Full refund** |
| After that, **until the trip's final payment date** | **50% refund** |
| **On or after the trip's final payment date** | **Non-refundable** |

- The **final payment date** is the trip's own final payment date, as shown on the trip's page (the cruise line's or supplier's deadline for that trip). On the site it comes from the trip's key dates (`{date:final_payment}`), so a date set specially for one trip is the one that applies.
- The 7-day full refund only applies when the trip is at least 120 days away on the day you paid. If you pay closer to the trip than that, the 50% rule applies from the start, until the final payment date.

## Changes of traveler
- You can **transfer** your place, and the planning fee you paid, **to a replacement traveler at no charge**. The cruise line's or supplier's own name-change rules and deadlines still apply to the booking itself.

## If the trip is cancelled or moved
- **Cancelled by CBGV or by the supplier** (for example the cruise line cancels the sailing): **full refund** of the planning fee.
- **Rescheduled** (moved to a new date): your planning fee **carries over** to the new date. If you **can't make the new date**, you get a **full refund** of the planning fee.

## How refunds are paid
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method** within **14 days** of the refund being agreed.

## Short version (for the price board)
> The CBGV planning fee is fully refundable within 7 days of paying, 50% refundable until {date:final_payment}, and non-refundable after that. Full refund if the trip is cancelled.

(`{date:final_payment}` is filled in with the trip's final payment date.)
