GATE 25 · TRAVEL DOCUMENTS

The fine print, folded neatly

Cancellation policy +
  • Base fares are non-refundable (taxes and fees excepted), though Voyage Protection may reimburse a cancellation for a covered reason. The schedule below applies to Essential, Premium and RockStar fares.
  • More than 120 days out: a 7-day grace period after booking for a full refund, deposit included.
  • After the grace period, until 120 days out: full refund of payments, except the deposit, which becomes Future Voyage Credit.
  • 119 to 45 days out: money paid becomes Future Voyage Credit, good for 1 year from the original sail date.
  • 44 days or less: payments are final, but you can send someone in your place up to 48 hours before sailing.
  • Taxes and fees are always refunded, whenever you cancel.
Cruise protection, built in +

Virgin Voyages Voyage Protection, administered by Aon Affinity and underwritten by Arch Insurance Company. Highlights:

  • Trip cancellation: up to 100% of your trip cost (max $50,000) for covered reasons, like you, a family member or travel companion getting sick or hurt.
  • Trip interruption: up to 150% of trip cost (max $75,000).
  • Trip delay: up to $1,000 ($250 a day) for delays of 6+ hours.
  • Emergency medical: up to $25,000 each for accident and sickness expenses, plus $50,000 medical evacuation.
  • Baggage: up to $1,500 for loss or damage, and $500 if bags are delayed 24+ hours on the way.
  • 24/7 emergency assistance from CareFree Travel Assistance: 1-877-303-5909 (U.S. & Canada).

Medical and baggage coverage may be secondary to other insurance you have. Coverage is subject to the policy's terms, conditions and exclusions, which vary by state of residence. Claims: aontravelclaim.com.

Payment plans & deadlines +
  • Final payment is due June 27, 2027, 120 days before sailing.
  • If a card is saved with Virgin, the balance is charged automatically that morning. Apple Pay and PayPal are not auto-charged, so pay those by the due date.
  • Pay in full any time before then to skip the auto-charge.
  • Flex Pay by Upgrade: book by Oct 25, 2026 (365+ days before sailing) and qualified sailors can get 0% APR over 12 months. Terms depend on credit; APRs range from 0% to 36% and not everyone is eligible.
  • Cruise protection must be added before final payment.
  • Booking after June 27, 2027? Full payment is due at booking.
ID required for boarding +
  • Bring a valid, government-issued passport. Virgin only accepts passports as ID for check-in.
  • Your passport name, birth date and nationality must match your booking.
  • Online check-in opens about 18 days before sailing (around Oct 7, 2027).
  • Linking a card at check-in places a temporary $250 hold, released after the voyage.
Special rates, accessibility & upgrades +
  • Florida residents, military and first responders may qualify for special Virgin rates. Let us know when you register and we'll check.
  • Need an accessible cabin? Virgin has accessible cabins in several categories. Tell us early so we can hold the right one.
  • Want an egg chair instead of a hammock? A few forward Sea Terraces have one. Ask us and we'll check which cabins.
  • Dreaming bigger? Virgin's Level Upgrade lets you bid on a better cabin in the weeks before sailing.
  • Name changes depend on your fare: none on Base, sailors 2–4 on Essential, everyone on Premium and RockStar (up to 48 hours before sailing). Date changes on Essential and up are allowed until 45 days out.
Miami hotels & flights +
  • Valiant Lady sails from Terminal V at PortMiami.
  • Pre- and post-cruise Miami hotel nights can be added to your booking. Tick "I need a hotel" when you register.
  • Need flights? Tick "I need a flight" and we'll book air once your cabin is paid in full.
