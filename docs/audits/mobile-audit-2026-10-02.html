Mobile audit — bagsandvibes.com

Read-only. Checked 2026-10-02 at 360, 375 and 414 px (phone) and 767 px (tablet proxy). Nothing was changed on the site.

Read this first: what this audit could and couldn't cover
  • The member session in the browser pane had expired (even on desktop, /dashboard/ redirected to the login form). I can't sign in for you, so every members-only page was only reachable as the sign-in message inside its page shell. Everything in the "Not reached" list at the bottom still needs a pass once you sign in.
  • 768 px exactly was blocked: SiteGround showed its robot-check CAPTCHA to the desktop-style browser the pane uses at that width. I did not solve it. I used 767 px (phone-style browser) instead. The site stylesheet has breakpoints at 540/640/860/900 px and none at 768, so 767 and 768 render the same for our own CSS; Kadence, Ultimate Member or WPForms could still have their own 768 rules.
  • The CAPTCHA appeared after roughly 70 page loads in about half an hour from the pane's connection. You may see it in that pane too; it is SiteGround's, and it is why I stopped there.
  • Measurements come from live computed styles on each page (overflow, font sizes, tap-target sizes with the closed menu filtered out, image shape); screenshots are from the same pane.

Broken

1. Homepage "Gate 05: Your One-Stop Shop" is clipped on phones

The headline starts 57 px off the left edge and the body card starts at x=278 and runs 57 px off the right edge, so words are cut mid-letter ("itinerari", "paymen"). It happens at 360, 375 and 414 px; at 767 px both boxes fit.

#boarding → .phase-copy.is-top-center.phase-copy--nowrap (L=-57, W=335) and .phase-copy.is-bottom-right (R=432, W=154)

2. Page-title ribbon pushes the page wider than the screen

On pages with a longer title the ribbon doesn't wrap: it is 381 px wide (Gate 07, "Pre-Planned Vacations") and 387 px wide (Helmets trip) on a 375 px screen. The browser stretches the page (reported width 401–406 px), the fixed header goes with it, and the MENU button's right border is cut off. Short titles are fine (Family & Friends and Gates 08–12 fit at 360, 375 and 414; Gate 07 and Helmets fit at 414). Trip titles are typed in by you, so any long title will do this.

4. Login / Register / Password-reset forms

  • Inputs use 15 px text, below the 16 px that stops iPhones from zooming the page in when a field is tapped. They are 40 px tall (450 px wide at 767).
  • Password reset's only field has a placeholder but no label.
  • The Login button is blue and Register is gray, off-brand next to the rest of the site.
  • Otherwise these pages fit the screen at every width, with no sideways scroll and full-width fields.

5. Logged-out visitors get the member menu

On the Gate pages and on Login/Register the menu shows Dashboard, My Profile, Feed, Navigation (all six Gates), Account and Logout, even to someone who isn't signed in. Every item leads to a sign-in prompt or a redirect, and a visitor sees Logout.

6. Open dropdown label disappears

When a menu dropdown (Navigation, Account…) is open and focused, its text turns the same gold as its background (both rgb(232,169,78)), so the label vanishes. The dropdowns themselves work: they open, close one another, and Escape closes them. The menu fits on a 667 px-tall phone.

7. Itinerary table on public trip pages

The table is 743 px wide inside a 295 px box that scrolls sideways. Day and Date show, but the Port column is cut off ("Puerto Plata, Do…", "Bimini Beach Clu…") and Country, Type, Time and Tender are hidden until the reader swipes. (The landing-page redesign replaces this for trips switched to the new design.)

8. Contact page has no side margin

Text, the email line and the form fields touch the screen edges (fields are 373 px wide on a 375 px screen); the Submit button is WPForms' default blue.

Cosmetic

  • Text under 12 px on every page: GATE tags and "JOIN US" (10.9 px), menu label, table headers, footer copyright (11.2 px), "Reserve Your Spot", "Members Login", "Register to Travel", "Departs from" (11.5 px).
  • Homepage header is transparent, so the logo and MENU overlap text and the GATE tags while you scroll (see the screenshot under Broken #1).
  • Escape closes a dropdown but not the menu itself.
  • Family & Friends itinerary data: Day 4 shows "Nov 29, 2027" and "Nov 26, 2027" and Day 5 shows "Nov 30, 2027" for an Oct 25–30 trip. That's in the trip's itinerary rows, not the template; you can fix it in wp-admin.
  • Aside: Gate 11 (Travel Rules) shows its full text to logged-out visitors, while the other Gates ask for sign-in. Fine if intended.

What checked out

  • No sideways scrolling at 360/375/414/767 px on: Home, Login, Register, Password reset, Privacy, Terms, Contact, Payment Disclaimer, Join a Trip, Family & Friends landing, Reaccept Terms, and Gates 08–12 (plus Gate 07 and Helmets at 414 and 767).
  • Viewport meta tag present everywhere; no stretched or broken images detected; the hamburger menu opens and closes; the homepage hero, Login, Register and trip hero images crop sensibly.

Suggested fix order

  1. Homepage Gate 05 clipping (Broken #1). The most visible bug on the front door.
  2. Page-title ribbon (Broken #2). One CSS rule in the shared shell fixes every Gate and trip page and any future long title.
  3. Tap targets in the shared header, menu and footer (#3). One change fixes all pages.
  4. Form fields to 16 px / 44 px, a label on the reset field, a bigger terms checkbox (#4).
  5. Logged-out menu (#5): show Home / Sign in / Register only.
  6. Dropdown label colour (#6). A one-line fix.
  7. Contact page gutter and form button colours (#8, #4).
  8. Minimum 12 px text and homepage header background on scroll (cosmetic batch).
  9. Itinerary table (#7). Skip it for trips moving to the redesigned landing page; otherwise stack the rows on phones.
  10. Fix the Family & Friends itinerary dates in wp-admin. No code needed, can be done today.

Not reached: still to audit

Members-only pages (need a signed-in session in the pane): Dashboard; the actual content of Gates 07, 08, 09 (Payments), 10 (Discussion Boards) and 12 (Vacation Requests); the private trip detail view (roster members only); the Payments page content; Profile / wall (/user/…); Account pages (/account/general/, password, travel profile, privacy, delete); Find Members (/members/) results; Feed (/member-feed/) and Following with real posts; individual forums, topics and replies; the trip Join form after registering; Reaccept Terms as a member.

Admin-only pages (can't be reached with a member session; please check yourself): wp-admin trip edit screens, including the new "New Landing Design" box; the Pricing Tiers, Itinerary and Invites meta boxes; Appointment Requests; Proposals and the Proposal PDF screens; Roster export; Membership Terms and Payment Disclaimer settings; the Trip Guests list; Yoast settings. These are desktop-first admin screens, but worth a quick phone look if you manage trips from a phone.
