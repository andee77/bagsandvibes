How booking works

  1. Become a member. Scan your trip's QR code or tap your invite link, then register. Joining through a friend's invite? You're in right away. Otherwise, we'll review your account and email you once you're approved.

  2. Choose your path.

    Join a planned trip: open the trip and complete the Trip Registration. Need flights, a hotel before or after the trip, or a rental car? Just tick the boxes, and your travel advisor gets the details.

    Plan a trip for your group: go to Gate 11 and complete a Vacation Request. Your coordinator will send you the trip details. Once you approve them, the trip goes live, and you'll get a link and QR code to share with your group.

  3. Reserve your spot. Pay your CBGV Group Experience Fee on the Payments page (Gate 09) in your Passport.

  4. Book with your travel advisor. Schedule an appointment, and your travel advisor will review every detail with you and book your travel.

Good to know about payments: Besides the CBGV Group Experience Fee, some trips need a deposit to hold your spot, depending on the cruise line, resort or vendor. Deposits and travel payments go to your travel advisor or the vendor, not to CBGV. You can pay in full, or pay a deposit and then monthly payments. For monthly payments, we recommend keeping a payment method on file with your travel advisor, so each payment is made on time.

Bookings are made by an independent travel advisor. See our Payment Disclaimer.
